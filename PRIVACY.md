# Medwi privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

Medwi is a medication reminder app for Android made by Butiá Labs. This policy explains what
happens to the information you enter in the app.

## Summary

Medwi does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no
analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not request internet access. Butiá Labs never
receives or sees what you store in the app.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter so it can remind you and show your history:

- medicines (name, strength, dose, pill shape and color, stock);
- schedules and reminder times;
- the history of doses taken, skipped or missed;
- app settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing
its data deletes it.

The medicine-name suggestions come from a list bundled inside the app. Searching it happens
on the device.

## Backups you control

Medwi only copies your data off the device in these cases, and each one is under your
control:

- **Android backup.** If backup to your Google account is turned on in your phone's settings,
  Android may include Medwi's data in that backup and restore it on a new phone. This is
  handled by Android and Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  Butiá Labs has no access to it.
- **Automatic backup file and export.** If you turn on the daily backup or export your data,
  Medwi writes a file to the location you pick (for example a folder on the phone or a cloud
  drive app). The file then follows the rules of the place you saved it to.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Show dose and refill reminders. |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | Make reminders go off at the scheduled minute. |
| Run at startup | Re-arm reminders after the phone restarts. |
| Vibration, keep awake | Vibrate and finish handling a reminder while the screen is off. |
| Biometrics | Optional app lock that uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN. Medwi never sees your biometric data. |

## Children

Medwi is not directed at children under 13.
