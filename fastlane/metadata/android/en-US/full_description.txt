Medwi reminds you to take your medicines on time and keeps a simple history of what you took. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection required.

<b>Reminders</b>
• Exact alarms that survive reboots, battery savers and Doze
• Sticky notifications with Take, Snooze and Skip, repeated until you answer
• "Take all" when several medicines share a time
• A checklist to fix the phone settings that block reminders (battery optimization, auto-start on Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, Oppo, Vivo and others)

<b>Schedules</b>
• Every day, every X days/weeks/months, specific weekdays, cycles (e.g. 21+7) or only when needed
• Automatic time organizer: N doses a day spread every 24/N hours; moving one time shifts the others
• Variable doses for tapering or titration

<b>Medicines</b>
• Strength (125 mg, 10 mg/ml…) and real dose units (pills, ml, drops, puffs, IU…)
• Pick the pill's shape and colors so you recognize it at a glance
• Stock tracking with refill reminders and "+1 box"
• Pause or end a treatment without losing its history
• Offline name suggestions from public U.S. (openFDA) and Brazilian (ANVISA) data

<b>History and backup</b>
• Monthly adherence calendar; tap a day to see and correct what was taken
• Home-screen widget with your next doses
• Optional app lock with your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN
• Android backup to your Google account, a daily automatic copy to a file you choose (for example in Google Drive), and manual export/import
